=== Approach 3: root-causing the 6 flagged "LARGE DIFF" cases from Approach 1 ===
All 6 flagged files are 3x3-pixel progressive images with one chroma-subsampled component
(2x2 or asymmetric) - i.e. a single 16x16-pixel MCU covering a 3x3 image, heavily padded,
with restart_interval in {1,2,4}.

Isolating the variable: re-tested gen2/g43.jpg's exact parameters (3x3, comp1 h=2 v=2, DRI=1)
against a 4:4:4 (no subsampling) variant of the same 3x3 image:

  $ convert -size 3x3 gradient:red-blue -sampling-factor "1x1,1x1,1x1" -interlace Plane \
        -define jpeg:restart-interval=1 /tmp/g43_444.jpg
  decode_test max diff vs libjpeg-turbo reference: 0

Re-tested the same 2x2-subsampled 3x3 image with restart interval removed entirely
(no DRI marker at all):

  $ convert -size 3x3 gradient:red-blue -sampling-factor "2x2,1x1,1x1" -interlace Plane \
        /tmp/g43_norestart.jpg
  decode_test max diff vs libjpeg-turbo reference: 55 (same magnitude as with DRI=1)

This isolates the diff to chroma-subsampling upsampling on a heavily-padded tiny image,
independent of restart interval, independent of progressive multi-scan structure (it's
already present with just the SOF sampling factor difference). It is a chroma-upsampling
filter difference (this crate's box/nearest-style upsampling vs. libjpeg-turbo's fancy
upsampling algorithm producing different edge-extrapolation results on a 3-pixel-wide image
padded out to a 16-pixel MCU), not a block-offset/index arithmetic defect - the values are
self-consistent within a row/column (no scrambled/misplaced block, just a different filtered
value at the same position), and it reproduces with plain SOF baseline sampling-factor
mismatch alone.
