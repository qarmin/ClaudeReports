All 3 runs aborted (SIGABRT, exit code 134) trying to allocate ~85.9 GB (65535 * 65535 * 20
components, the 20 coming from the PoC's /DeviceN colorant-name array) from a 1261-byte PDF whose
page itself is an ordinary 200x200pt MediaBox. Note: with a plain 3-component (/DeviceRGB) image
instead of the /DeviceN trick, the same missing cap still requests a very real ~12.88 GB
allocation (65535 * 65535 * 3) that this particular 29GB-RAM/8GB-swap test host's allocator
happens to satisfy (confirmed separately: RSS peaked at ~12.6 GB, ~15s wall-clock, for a 3-run-only
side observation, not part of this candidate's official repeat-run evidence) - the /DeviceN variant
above is used for the official repro because it reliably aborts on any real machine regardless of
its total RAM+swap, without relying on an artificial `ulimit`.
