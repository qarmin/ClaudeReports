=== Notes on the "stray Item followed by delimiters" trace, pristine tree ===

Only the first token (the bogus `SequenceStart` for tag (FFFE,E000)) is the primary defect this
byte sequence demonstrates in `LazyDataSetReader`. The remaining trace (`SequenceEnd`, `ItemEnd`,
`SequenceEnd`) differs from the equivalent trace against the eager `DataSetReader` in candidate
050's repro (which manufactures a *second* bogus element from the same bytes) because
`LazyDataSetReader`'s Item Delimitation handling (prior to this fix) unconditionally pops
`seq_delimiters` and flips `in_sequence = true` without checking whether anything was actually
open - so the trailing `(FFFE,E00D)` bytes are absorbed into an incorrect but merely confusing
`ItemEnd` token (not a reserved-tag element) rather than falling through to the generic
element-header catch-all as they do in the eager reader. The `try_read` helper in this repro
still correctly reports `bug_a = true` from the first bogus `SequenceStart` token alone. The
second, independent gap (a stray `SequenceDelimiter` on its own falling into the generic
element-header catch-all) is demonstrated cleanly and directly by the second, standalone input.
