=== Mechanism confirmation (temporary instrumentation, reverted afterward) ===
A temporary eprintln! added at the `packet.track_id != serial` arm in
symphonia-format-ogg/src/demuxer.rs's do_seek() (in a scratch worktree, never committed/left in
the real tree) printed, for this same repro input/target (track 0x344085ff, t=1.07s, seekable File
source):

DEBUG013: cross-stream packet seen: page_serial(peeked-from)=0x769f9c6f packet.track_id=0x769f9c6f seek_target_serial=0x344085ff pts=-128
DEBUG013: cross-stream packet seen: page_serial(peeked-from)=0x769f9c6f packet.track_id=0x769f9c6f seek_target_serial=0x344085ff pts=-128
DEBUG013: cross-stream packet seen: page_serial(peeked-from)=0x769f9c6f packet.track_id=0x769f9c6f seek_target_serial=0x344085ff pts=-128
DEBUG013: confirmed spin (200 identical iterations), aborting

This confirms that after the seekable bisection converges on a page belonging to track
0x344085ff, that page's own buffered packets are exhausted before target_ts is reached, the loop's
`_ => match self.read_page()` arm reads the next physical page (which belongs to the *other*
logical stream, 0x769f9c6f), and the loop then spins on that peeked-but-never-discarded
cross-stream packet forever - reached entirely through the seekable/bisection fast path
(`is_seekable() == true`), no ReadOnlySource / non-seekable wrapper involved.
