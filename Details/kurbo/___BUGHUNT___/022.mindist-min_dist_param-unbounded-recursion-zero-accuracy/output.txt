=== Evidence that flooring `epsilon` alone (without a depth cap) is insufficient ===

A first fix attempt only clamped `epsilon` to a small positive floor (e.g. `epsilon.max(1e-9)`).
This does stop the literal crash for `accuracy = 0.0` on the two axis-aligned parallel Lines used
above (bounded recursion depth ~30, terminates fast: prints `result: 5.0`). But probing the same
`min_dist_param` termination check directly with a *slightly skewed* pair of Lines and a range of
small-but-positive `epsilon` values shows the underlying subdivision search itself does very
little effective pruning for near-parallel/near-symmetric input - total work scales close to
O(1/epsilon), not O(log(1/epsilon)):

    eps=0.1     result: 4.7909419741842      in 183.808µs
    eps=0.01    result: 4.789234550335262    in 1.654344ms
    eps=0.001   result: 4.789131500095557    in 13.293628ms
    eps=0.0001  result: 4.789131430476778    in 208.723885ms
    eps=0.00001 result: 4.789131426228914    in 1.964530676s
    eps=0.000001 result: 4.789131426107847   in 14.275514552s

And with the exact original repro (accuracy = 0.0, epsilon floored to 1e-9 only, no depth cap):
still did not return within 180s (`timeout 180 ./mindist_repro` exited 124 - timed out, not
crashed). This is why the shipped fix (`patch.diff`) adds a hard recursion-depth backstop
(`MAX_DEPTH = 12`, independent of `epsilon`'s value) rather than only flooring `epsilon`.
