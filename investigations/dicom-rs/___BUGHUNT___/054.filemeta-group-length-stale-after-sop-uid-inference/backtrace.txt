=== Provenance ===

Found by temporarily stacking candidates 001, 002, 008, 048, 049, 050, 051, 052, 053's already-
confirmed patches onto the tree and re-running the existing `object/fuzz/fuzz_targets/open_file.rs`
for 150s (`ASAN_OPTIONS=symbolize=0 cargo +nightly fuzz run open_file -- -max_total_time=150`),
which turned up a crashing input (`cargo +nightly fuzz tmin` minimized it to 340 bytes). A
parse/write/re-parse/compare diagnostic, combined with a byte-offset walk of the actually-written
meta group (bypassing the WRITTEN file's own declared, and as it turned out wrong,
`information_group_length` to see where each element really started/ended), traced the
discrepancy precisely: `object/src/meta.rs`'s `FileMetaTable::build()` computes
`information_group_length` once, from whatever fields were populated purely from the wire; the
enrichment step in `object/src/mem.rs` (candidate 053) then mutates
`media_storage_sop_class_uid`/`media_storage_sop_instance_uid` *afterward*, without ever calling
`update_information_group_length()` again - unlike every other place in this codebase that mutates
a meta field directly (`set_transfer_syntax`, `apply()`), both of which already call it
immediately after their own mutation. The minimal, hand-built reproduction here isolates the
defect directly and was confirmed to reproduce fully independently of every other stacked
candidate, including 053 itself (tested against a pristine tree with *only* this candidate's
patch applied, with no dependency on 053's SOP-UID-padding fix).
