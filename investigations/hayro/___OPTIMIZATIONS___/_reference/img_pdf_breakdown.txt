Glyph outline sub-breakdown (draw_glyph -> fill_glyph -> cached_outline):
  1232 glyph draw calls per render.
  cache-hit lookup:            ~4.4-4.9 ms / 100 iter  (~3.2% of draw_glyph time)
  cache-miss (extract outline): ~9.8-10.9 ms / 100 iter (~7.6% of draw_glyph time)
  => ~89% of draw_glyph's cost is NOT outline management - it's the
     vello_cpu `ctx.fill_path()` call itself (one real fill per glyph
     instance; outlines are cached but each occurrence still needs its own
     rasterization pass since screen position differs).
