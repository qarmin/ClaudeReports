Candidate 028 - RemoteDebuggerPeerTCP::has_message()/is_peer_connected() missing lock
======================================================================================

Setup: a real second participant (server_project/, a genuine TCP debug server speaking the
real var_to_bytes()-encoded wire protocol) connects over a real loopback socket to the real
debuggee (client_project/), started with `--remote-debug tcp://127.0.0.1:17891` against a
real, self-built `godot.linuxbsd.template_debug.x86_64.san` binary
(scons platform=linuxbsd target=template_debug use_tsan=yes debug_symbols=yes
disable_path_overrides=no). The server floods ~40-90k real length-prefixed `var_to_bytes([])`
messages over ~12s while the debuggee just idles (its own per-frame debugger polling already
exercises the racy accessors), then disconnects to flip `connected`.
