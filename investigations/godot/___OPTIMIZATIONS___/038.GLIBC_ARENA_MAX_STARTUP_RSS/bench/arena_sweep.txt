Reading: the ~57MB saving is essentially binary, not proportional to the arena cap. It appears
reliably only at MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=1; at 2-4 the same run-to-run bimodality shows up with ~57MB
amplitude (whether the extra arenas get created at all depends on thread timing), and at 8+ /
default it never appears. There is therefore no intermediate "sweet spot": every setting that
sometimes avoids the arenas also costs startup time, and only 1 avoids them every time.

Godot's own tracked counters are byte-identical across all settings
(usage=91165395 max=448283037), confirming none of this memory is engine allocation - it is glibc
per-thread arena space, invisible to Memory::get_mem_usage()/get_mem_max_usage().
