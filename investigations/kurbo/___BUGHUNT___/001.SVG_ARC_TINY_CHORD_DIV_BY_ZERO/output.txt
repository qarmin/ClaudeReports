=== Release-mode behavior on pristine tree (debug_assert compiled out -> silent NaN/data corruption) ===

$ cargo run --release
OK, path = BezPath([MoveTo((0.0, 0.0))])

Note: the release build does not panic (debug_assert! is a no-op in release), but the div-by-zero
at svg.rs:471-473 (`coe * rxpy / ry`, `-coe * rypx / rx`) produces a NaN-filled `Arc`. Because
`sweep_angle`'s NaN makes every comparison in `Arc::to_cubic_beziers`'s internal loop condition
false, no `CurveTo` is ever emitted for the arc segment - it is silently dropped from the parsed
path instead of being replaced by even a degenerate line, unlike the `is_straight_line()` case.
