## Where the time actually goes (perf, 2026-09-02, `taskset -c 2`, `-F 999 -g`, release+debug=true)

Flat profiles of the four heaviest `kurbo_bench` scenes, for whoever picks a candidate next:

| scene | top symbols (self %) |
|---|---|
| `stroke` | `CubicOffset::eval_err` **55.0%**, `CubicOffset::offset_rec` 20.9%, `CubicOffset::apply` 8.5%, ~~`memmove` 4.7%~~ **corrected 2026-09-02 by candidate 018: memmove is 1.08%, and 92.6% of it is `offset_rec` copying the 264-byte `ErrEval` struct, not `BezPath` buffer growth - the 4.7% figure sent 018 after the wrong buffer**, `__ieee754_atan2` 2.6%, `solve_itp` 1.7%, `__sincos` 1.5% |
| `offset` | `CubicOffset::eval_err` **62.7%**, `CubicOffset::offset_rec` 17.3%, `CubicOffset::apply` 9.3%, `solve_itp` 3.2%, `__ieee754_atan2` 2.8% |
| `flatten` | `QuadBez::estimate_subdiv` **40.7%**, `bezpath::flatten` 35.3%, `CubicBez::subsegment` 23.9% |
| `fit` | `__ieee754_atan2` 17.6% (+`__atan2` 3.2%), `CurveDist::eval_ray` 12.7%, `solve_cubic` 10.6%, `cbrt` (compiler_builtins) 10.0%, `__sincos` 8.0%, `fit_to_cubic` 6.0%, `factor_quartic_inner` 3.8% |

Headline: **`offset::CubicOffset::eval_err` is the single dominant cost in both stroking and
offsetting** - stroke expansion is essentially "call `offset_cubic` a lot", so anything that makes
`eval_err` cheaper moves the two biggest real-world kurbo entry points at once.
