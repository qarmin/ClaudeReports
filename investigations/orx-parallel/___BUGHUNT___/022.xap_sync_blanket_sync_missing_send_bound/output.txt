Result: 10/10 STILL reproduces after the attempted fix, same magnitude as before the patch. See
RESULT.md ("The fix") for why: the race is on the pointee of a raw pointer captured by the
user-supplied closure, which is identically aliased whether the pointer is shared via one
instance's `&self` (the pre-patch XapSync design) or duplicated into a bitwise Copy per real
worker thread (the patched design, matching every sibling combinator) - Cell/RefCell/UnsafeCell
are all `!Copy` in std, so no safe (non-`unsafe impl Send`-asserting) Copy + Send-but-!Sync type
exists whose observable behavior would actually differ between the two designs.
