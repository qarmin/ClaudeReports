(Note: a pure *uniform* scale transform that overflows f32 produces scale_x=scale_y=1e-6, not
inf - because s1-s2 becomes inf-inf=NaN for a uniform scale, and f32::max(NaN, 1e-6) absorbs the
NaN into the floor (this is candidate 019's already-confirmed, separate bug). A *non-uniform*
scale (huge on one axis only) avoids that cancellation and genuinely produces scale_x=inf, which
is this candidate's target.)
