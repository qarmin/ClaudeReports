(This is the correct fixed behavior: `MemorySettings::normalize` now caps `max_atlases` at 256 -
the packed format's real 8-bit `atlas_index` domain - regardless of how much array-layer headroom
the device itself reports, so the 257th atlas allocation is honestly rejected with a typed
`AtlasLimitReached` error instead of silently succeeding and corrupting the render. The panic here
comes from `Renderer::upload_image`'s own pre-existing internal `.unwrap()` on that `Result` - a
separate, pre-existing API design choice in the convenience wrapper, not something introduced by
this patch or this repro.)
