failures:

    tile::tests::horizontal_line_right_to_left_three_tile
    tile::tests::resize_works_correctly
    tile::tests::same_tile_right

(All 5 are horizontal, non-tile-aligned lines - a *second*, independent defect in the same dead
branch: for horizontal lines `x_slope = dx/dy = dx/0.0 = +-infinity`, and
`row_top_x = p0_x + (row_top_y - p0_y) * x_slope` computes `0.0 * infinity = NaN` whenever
`row_top_y == p0_y` exactly. The always-executed "clamped" branch happens to rescue this via
`f32::min/max` discarding the NaN operand in favor of `line_left_x`/`line_right_x`; the "unclamped"
branch has no such rescue and collapses to a spurious `x=0` tile, exactly as check 2 above showed
for the NaN-coordinate-input case.)

This is why the shipped fix removes the dead branch entirely rather than attempting to repair
"needs_clamping" into a real fast path - Step B shows that a naive, well-intentioned fix restoring
the check's literal documented intent (comparing against viewport bounds) is not sufficient on its
own; the branch has a second, independent defect.
