| 2026-09-30 | Issue #155 - zune-jpeg IDCT/dequant optimization ideas (AVX2 hand-written vs auto-vec, 2-block-at-a-time, fold dequant into IDCT, zigzag-fused transpose) | Still open. Detailed performance report from a contributor who benchmarked zune-jpeg's IDCT: the scalar zero-coefficient-skip loop auto-vectorizes worse than a rewritten version (godbolt link in issue), the hand-written AVX2 IDCT may not even beat the auto-vectorized scalar path, AVX2 could process 2 blocks at once (16x u16 lanes), dequantization could be fused into the IDCT to avoid register/memory roundtrips, and one transpose could be eliminated by doing it during zigzag unscrambling instead of after. This spawned draft PR #157 (10% faster, but currently produces corrupted images - needs the IDCT itself adapted, not just the data layout). Concrete, hot-path, well-reasoned ideas worth re-deriving/benchmarking independently in src/idct/ and src/mcu*.rs. | https://github.com/etemesi254/zune-image/issues/155 (see also draft PR https://github.com/etemesi254/zune-image/pull/157) |
| 2026-09-30 | PR #435 - decode JPEG MCUs via a precomputed block list instead of nested scan-order loops (draft, perf benchmark PR) | Open draft PR explicitly opened to benchmark a hot-path change: building a flat list of blocks in iteration order (rather than re-deriving offsets via scan-order nested loops each iteration) so indexing collapses to `base + stride * j_mcu`, which the author says helps progressive images via constant folding. Also surfaces a spec-compliance gap (B.2.1: non-DC scans must not have >1 component) uncovered while refactoring. Worth checking the benchmark numbers and diff for applicability to this project's own JPEG decode loop restructuring ideas. | https://github.com/etemesi254/zune-image/pull/435 |

Directly a hot-path perf feature (skips IDCT and color-convert work proportionally to the scale factor) worth checking correctness/speed even ahead of merge. | https://github.com/etemesi254/zune-image/issues/434 and https://github.com/etemesi254/zune-image/pull/412 |

A genuine hot-path perf feature (avoids full-image IDCT + color conversion for viewport-only decode of huge images) worth checking/benchmarking. Related: issue #418 (tissue-slide-scanner use case) and overlapping PR #414. | https://github.com/etemesi254/zune-image/pull/411 (related: https://github.com/etemesi254/zune-image/issues/418, https://github.com/etemesi254/zune-image/pull/414) |
