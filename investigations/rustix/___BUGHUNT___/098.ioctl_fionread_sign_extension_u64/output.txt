Confirms the kernel's real FIONREAD behavior on a 3,000,000,000-byte sparse regular file: it writes
back a raw c_int of -1294967296, whose bit pattern reinterpreted as u32 correctly recovers
3000000000 (the documented "wrapped value" rustix's own doc comment promises) - rustix's bug is
purely in how it widens that already-wrapped c_int into a u64 (sign-extends instead of
zero-extending through u32).
